Our Foundation

Care Rooted in Compassion

For over 125 years, Christian Medical College Vellore has stood as a beacon of hope — where faith, service, and medical excellence converge to transform lives.

Founded on the principle "Not to be ministered unto, but to minister," we serve millions with dignity, compassion, and world-class clinical care.
